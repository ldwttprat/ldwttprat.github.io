Mt. Fuji as Window to Japan

University of Tokyo · Spring 2016 & 2017

For the inaugural thematic course “Global Praxis” of the exchange program between the University of Tokyo and Australia National University, I designed a series of lectures discussing various aspects of Japan through the window of Mt. Fuji.

My lectures in 2016 and 2017 focused on sacred space, pilgrimage, and gender.
