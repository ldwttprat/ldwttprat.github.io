Bio

Lindsey DeWitt Prat moved from windswept Wyoming to Paris by way of Japan—years on sacred mountains and classical texts, then a jump from Buddhist studies professor to industry research leader. With a background in global politics, premodern Asian religions, visual culture, ritual technologies, and placemaking, Lindsey’s practice is fundamentally multidimensional. She is currently a Director of Research at Bold Insight, an EPIC (epicpeople.org) Board Member, an Adjunct Senior Research Fellow in Linguistics at the University of Western Australia, and a 2026–27 Fellow of Utrecht University’s Inclusive AI Lab.

Short bio

Lindsey DeWitt Prat works on the gap between processing and understanding in technology systems, and the contextual nuances required to close it. With a background in global politics, premodern Asian religions, visual culture, ritual technologies, and placemaking, her practice is fundamentally multidimensional. She is currently a Director of Research at Bold Insight, an EPIC Board Member, and an Adjunct Senior Research Fellow in Linguistics at the University of Western Australia.
